You’ll have to pardon my lack of posts these past few months – I’ve been busy finishing up my Legal Letters Master in Insurance Law at the University of Connecticut. I’m happy to announce that I graduated May 20, and the ceremony was absolutely beautiful and inspiring.

I’ve attended my share of graduations – both my own and others’ – and this was the best graduation I’ve seen thus far. We do owe thanks to Mother Nature, for she provided a sunny day with perfect temperatures, but we were all quite nervous the day before, as a severe thunderstorm was forecasted right up until the last minute!



So what does this mean for MisInsured? I will now have more time to dedicate to content, and you will (hopefully) see my publications in other mediums, such as Law Journals and in full-length book format. The future is bright, and I look forward to continuing to dedicate my time toward education for insurance consumers, in my attempt to make a little difference in this world! Hope you are all having a wonderful summer so far (and if it isn’t summer where you are, I hope you are healthy and enjoying whatever weather comes your way – may it be favorable!).
