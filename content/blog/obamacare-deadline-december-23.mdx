If you haven’t already enrolled in [Obamacare](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patient_Protection_and_Affordable_Care_Act) but want to be covered by January 1, you need to take a look at your state’s exchange before December 23. For those of you who are still uncertain that the ACA coverage is for you – I’ve located a website that is an excellent place to start. U.S. News and World Report has compiled state-specific, easy-to-read guides in their [Health Insurance Section.](http://health.usnews.com/health-insurance)

Good luck to everyone!
