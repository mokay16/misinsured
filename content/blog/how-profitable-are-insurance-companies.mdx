I was reading a publication of the [Federal Insurance Office](http://www.treasury.gov/about/organizational-structure/offices/Pages/Federal-Insurance.aspx) (a division of the U.S. Department of the Treasury), and was shocked to learn that *half of all U.S. bank-held assets belong to insurance companies.* As of the end of 2012, the Life/Health and Property/Casualty insurance industries held $7.3 trillion in total assets. The total amount of assets held by FDIC-insured institutions in 2012 was [$14.5 trillion](https://www2.fdic.gov/qbp/2012dec/qbp.pdf). Can you believe insurance companies [recently claimed they were not “systemically important”](https://kathleendefever.wordpress.com/2013/04/20/insurance-companies-struggle-to-argue-they-are-not-important/) in the United States…?

For more on the Federal Insurance Office’s recommendations for improving the U.S. system of insurance regulation, see “[How to Modernize and Improve the System of Insurance Regulation in the United States](http://www.treasury.gov/initiatives/fio/reports-and-notices/Documents/How%20to%20Modernize%20and%20Improve%20the%20System%20of%20Insurance%20Regulation%20in%20the%20United%20States.pdf),” December 2013.

For more on the regulation of “Systemically Important” insurance companies, see “[The Nine Insurers That Pose a Threat to the Global Economy](https://kathleendefever.wordpress.com/2013/09/18/the-nine-insurers-that-pose-a-threat-to-the-global-economy/).”
