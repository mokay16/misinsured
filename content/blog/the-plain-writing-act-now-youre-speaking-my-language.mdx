*KISS: Keep It Simple, Stupid (photo: Wikipedia)*



On this blog I strive to keep insurance terms and concepts SIMPLE. I firmly believe that insurance doesn’t have to be so darned complicated.

Apparently the Obama Administration also prefers to keep life simple. On October 13, 2010 President Obama signed “[The Plain Writing Act](http://www.gpo.gov/fdsys/pkg/PLAW-111publ274/pdf/PLAW-111publ274.pdf)“. The Plain Writing Act requires federal agencies to use “clear Government communication that the public can understand and use.” They’ve even created a nifty website with free advice on how to write and speak in simpler language. [Plainlanguage.gov](http://www.plainlanguage.gov/howto/index.cfm) provides links to dictionaries and style guides for assistance.
