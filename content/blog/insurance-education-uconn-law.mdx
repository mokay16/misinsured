*UConn Law Library*



Last week, I had the pleasure of visiting the [University of Connecticut School of Law](http://www.law.uconn.edu/). UConn is not only beautiful, but houses the [Insurance Law Center](http://www.insurancelawcenter.org/), the most impressive collection of insurance knowledge in the United States.



I hope you enjoy my amateur photos of UConn Law’s campus in [Hartford – the insurance capital of the world](http://www.livinglocal360.com/index.php/how-hartford-connecticut-became-insurance-capital-of-the-world-6886/).

And remember, if you have any insurance questions or wish to locate an insurance book, text, or resource of any kind, the [UConn law library](https://library.law.uconn.edu/node) is a fantastic resource.

*Uconn library books*





I also highly recommend the [Insurance Law LLM degree](http://www.insurancelawcenter.org/llm-program), taught by distinguished faculty with a wealth of experience in the insurance industry and its related fields.
