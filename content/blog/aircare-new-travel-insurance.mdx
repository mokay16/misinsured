Warren Buffett has announced his company Berkshire Hathaway will back a [new travel insurance called AirCare](http://abc7.com/travel/aircare-insurance-pays-cash-for-travel-inconveniences/234548/). We know Mr. Buffett to be quite the savvy investor, so why is he backing this particular travel insurance?

[AirCare’s benefits](https://www.bhtp.com/travel-protection/aircare) are attractive. The coverage will cost $25 per trip, and the following benefits are available:


- $50 if your flight is delayed more than 2 hours
- $500 if a delay causes you to miss your connecting flight or your bags are missing for more than 12 hours
- $1000 if your plane is stuck on the runway for more than 2 hours (with you inside) or your luggage is completely lost



According to George Hobica of [AirfareWatchdog.com](http://www.airfarewatchdog.com/), you get ***instant cash*** if your bags are missing. The words instant cash do sound attractive, indeed. However, as in all cases with insurance, don’t forget that the $25 you pay for the insurance will be “instantly” in the AirCare coffers, whether you experience travel difficulties or not… and [odds are, you won’t](http://blog.wandr.me/2014/05/dont-be-fooled-by-aircare-travel-insurance/) experience anything out of the ordinary. That’s what AirCare and Warren Buffett are betting on.
