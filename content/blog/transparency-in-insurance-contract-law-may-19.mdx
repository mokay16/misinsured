Webinar details:

Over the course of two moderated discussions, panellists will consider the most interesting challenges posed in the digital era to transparency obligations of the insurer in insurance contract law and practice. The different approaches in Europe and the US will undoubtedly lead to a lively debate.

Each discussion will be followed by a moderated Q&A session. **Enforcement of transparency requirements: 2.45pm-3.30pm** (5:45am-6:30am PST / 8:45am- 9:30am EST)

**Panellists :Kathleen Defever**, Owner/Principal of Defever Law and Public Insurance Adjuster, USA **Sjoerd Meijer,** Partner, Nauta Dutilh Amsterdam and Lecturer VU Amsterdam, Netherlands **Moderator:** **Joasia Luzak,** Associate Professor, University of Exeter, UK
