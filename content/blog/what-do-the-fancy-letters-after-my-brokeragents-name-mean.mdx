*Insurance Titles: Purely Elementary*



CLU: [Chartered Life Underwriter](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chartered_Life_Underwriter#Curriculum). This is the premier credential for life insurance agents, and takes 2-5 years of study to obtain.

ChFC: [Chartered Financial Consultant](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chartered_Financial_Consultant). This is the premier credential for financial planners, and takes 2-5 years of study (nine college level courses) and three years of experience to obtain.

CFP: [Certified Financial Planner](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certified_Financial_Planner). Similar to the ChFC. Bachelor’s Degree and passage of a 10 hour examination are required.

CPCU: [Chartered Property and Casualty Underwriter](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPCU). Equivalent to the CLU, but for property/casualty insurance. Requires nine graduate/post-graduate level courses, extra study of the code of ethics, and a minimum of three years of experience in the field.
