*Belgium Red Devils*



I haven’t been blogging due to a particularly super-busy month (yes I just made that word up). Besides my regular legal work, I’ve started writing for Findlaw.com. I now realize that I need to rethink my MisInsured blogging strategy, i.e. shorter posts so I can post more often… So, for your enjoyment in the meantime, here you are – a short post that has practically nothing to do with insurance!

Belgium for the World Cup win! Sure, they are not a popular choice, they’ve never won, and no one ever thinks about tiny Belgium. But, the majority of my ancestors are Belgian and I ADORE this friendly, beautiful, cultured little country (special shout out to my relatives in Gent!). So there. I blogged.
